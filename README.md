Regional Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales, profit, and revenue performance across regions, product categories, and products for 2024 and 2025.

🎯 Objectives

Track revenue, profit, and sales growth.

Compare monthly and quarterly sales.

Analyze regional and product performance.

Identify growth opportunities and profitability gaps.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Power BI

Business Intelligence

Data Visualization

📈 Dashboard Features

KPI Cards: Total Profit, Total Revenue, Profit Margin, and Sales Growth.

Monthly Trends: Revenue comparison for 2024 and 2025.

Quarterly Analysis: Compare revenue across four quarters.

Category Analysis: Revenue distribution by product category.

Regional Analysis: Compare sales across five regions.

Product Analysis: Identify top revenue-generating products.

🔍 Key Insights

Total Revenue: 231.64M

Total Profit: 78.20M

Sales Growth: 17.1%

Electronics and Furniture contribute approximately 65% of revenue.

South region sales declined by 8.7%.

Q4 is the highest-revenue quarter in both years.

Electronics profit margin is 31.9%, below the 33.8% average.

💡 Recommendations

Improve South region sales.

Increase Electronics profitability.

Focus on high-performing products and categories.

Use sales trends for better planning.

🏆 Project Outcomes

Analyzed overall sales and profitability.

Identified regional and product performance trends.

Highlighted growth opportunities.

Supported data-driven business decisions.
